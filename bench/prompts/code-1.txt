Implement a thread-safe LRU cache in C++17 as a header-only class template `LruCache<Key, Value>` with a fixed capacity given at construction. It must support `std::optional<Value> get(const Key&)`, `void put(const Key&, const Value&)`, `bool erase(const Key&)`, `size_t size() const`, and O(1) average time for get and put. Use a std::list and std::unordered_map, protect every public method with a std::mutex, and include a small main() that exercises eviction order with capacity 3. Explain the eviction policy in two sentences after the code.
