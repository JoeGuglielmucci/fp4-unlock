Imagine a huge kitchen with 10,000 chefs. Each chef can chop, stir, and plate dishes incredibly fast. But all the ingredients are in a pantry across the room, and only one narrow hallway connects the pantry to the kitchen. If the chefs are faster than the hallway can deliver ingredients, the kitchen’s output is limited by the hallway, not by the chefs. That hallway is memory bandwidth.
