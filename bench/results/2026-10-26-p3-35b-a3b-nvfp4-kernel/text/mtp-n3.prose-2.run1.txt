   - **Recipient:** Hardware vendor

   - **Key Content Requirements:**
     - State what the company builds (an open-source inference engine)
     - Explain why early access matters before launch
     - State what the company offers in return (benchmarks, bug reports, a public write-up)
     - Propose a specific next step

   - **Format:** Formal letter

2.  **Deconstruct Requirements & Plan Structure:**

   - Closing: Professional sign-off
   - Word count target: ~350 words
